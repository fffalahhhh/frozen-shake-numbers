Spacing & surfaces

Page gutters: 20 px on mobile.

Spacing rhythm: 8, 10, 12, 16–17, 20, 24, 28 px.

Corner radius: 20 px shared token; cards 17–19 px; balance hero 24 px; controls 13–16 px.

Cards: white surface, 1 px soft border, no heavy shadow.

Icons: simple outline icons; iOS tab bar uses SF Symbols, other platforms use matching outline icons.

06 · Page structure

Navigation & brand use

Bottom navigation: Today, History, Partners, Categories. Use a simple four-item bottom bar with icon and label; highlight the active tab in shop green.

Today: date selector → cash summary → three quick actions → revenue row → expense list.

Partners: prominent “Record cash handover” button → partner-total grid → recent handovers.

Categories: add-category input → active/hidden category lists → partner names → local-storage note.

Logo: the uploaded Frozen Shake logo is applied as the mobile app icon and splash image. It is not shown in the in-app page headers.

Brand tone: clear, friendly, practical; prioritise amounts and touch-friendly controls over decoration.
